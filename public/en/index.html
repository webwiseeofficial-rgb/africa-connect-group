Who we are

A trusted gateway to doing business in Africa.

Africa Connect Group SARL is part of a drive for economic development, strategic advisory, business facilitation, international trade and partnership creation across Africa.

Our ambition: to add value to Africa’s wealth, support businesses and entrepreneurs, and build lasting commercial relationships between African players and international partners.

More about us →

Our vision

To become a reference company in connecting economic opportunities, adding value to African resources and developing strategic partnerships between Africa and the rest of the world.

Our mission

  • Support companies in their development and commercial strategy.
  • Facilitate business relationships between producers, suppliers, buyers, investors and partners.
  • Contribute to the value-addition of African raw materials.
  • Promote export opportunities and access to international markets.
