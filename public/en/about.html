Who we are

Africa Connect Group SARL is a company engaged in economic development, strategic advisory, business facilitation, international trade and partnership creation in Africa.

Our ambition is to help add value to Africa’s wealth, to support businesses and entrepreneurs, and to develop lasting commercial relationships between African economic players and international partners.

We are anchored in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC), with a vision to expand to other African markets, notably Southern Africa, and to international markets.

Our vision

To make Africa Connect Group a reference company in connecting economic opportunities, adding value to African resources and developing strategic partnerships between Africa and the rest of the world.

Our mission

  • Support companies in their development and commercial strategy.
  • Facilitate business relationships between producers, suppliers, buyers, investors and partners.
  • Contribute to the value-addition of African raw materials.
  • Promote export opportunities and access to international markets.
  • Facilitate commercial and institutional partnerships.
  • Help SMEs and entrepreneurs structure and grow their projects.
  • Promote sustainable, value-creating economic initiatives in Africa.

Our positioning

A business partner able to bring together opportunities, skills, resources and markets.

More than an online presence, we are building a genuine institutional showcase that inspires trust, makes contact easy and opens the way to new collaborations.

Our values

Trust

Business relationships built on transparency and reliability.

Partnership

Lasting collaborations that work for every party.

Openness

An international outlook, rooted in Africa.

Responsibility

Value creation that respects regulations and resources.

Our long-term ambition

Strengthen our presence across several African markets, develop international partnerships, support resource value-addition projects and contribute to creating sustainable economic opportunities.
